Level Vibes brand guide

Tap your energy. It picks the thing.  ·  Last updated 9 November 2026

The idea

Level Vibes is a gentle app for low-energy days, made by one person with ADHD who’s autistic. You tap the energy you’ve got (Level 1, 2 or 3) and it gives you one thing to do. The brand should feel like the app: warm, honest, a little funny, never preachy. Every level counts.

How to use them

  • The three level colours always mean the same thing: green = Level 1, yellow = Level 2, coral = Level 3. Keep them in that order (green, yellow, coral) whenever they appear together.
  • Charcoal is the home background for most posts. Cream is the light alternative (and the notebook-style ads use a warmer paper, #FBF8F1).
  • On green, yellow or coral, use charcoal text. On charcoal, use cream text.
  • Yellow text on cream is too pale to read: use the darker “as text” versions above.

Logo

The logo is the hand-drawn “Level Vibes” squiggle. Files are in Logo/: SVG (sharp at any size; best for Canva and print), PNG transparent, and PNG with background (square for profile pictures, wide for banners).

Do
  • Coral on charcoal is the main version.
  • On cream, use the darker coral (#D63F3F) or charcoal.
  • Leave space around it: at least the height of the “L”.
Don’t
  • Stretch, squash, rotate or outline it.
  • Put coral logo on coral, or any colour on a busy photo without a solid patch behind it.
  • Redraw or retype it in a font.

Voice

Write like Devon talks to a friend who’s having a hard day: honest, warm, a bit funny, never pushy.

Sounds like Level Vibes
  • “Some days one load of laundry is a win.”
  • “Every level counts.”
  • “No pressure. No guilt.”
  • “Getting started is the hardest part.”
  • “Cereal counts. Dinner is dinner.”
  • “I made it for me. Maybe it’s for you too.”
Doesn’t
  • Hustle talk: “crush your goals”, “no excuses”, “level up your productivity”.
  • Guilt or shame: “stop being lazy”.
  • Health promises: “fixes your ADHD”, “cures procrastination”.
  • Made-up numbers: “join thousands…” (only real numbers).

Things to keep true

  • The hard part is getting started (not just deciding). Level Vibes gives you a place to start.
  • Lead with Devon’s story. “I have ADHD and I’m autistic…” has been the best-performing message by far.
  • Streaks: the app does show streaks in History (Plus), so say “no pressure” or “nothing breaks if you skip a day”, not “no streaks”.
  • In ads (Meta’s rules): talk about yourself (“I have ADHD”), never about the viewer (“Do you have ADHD?”, “Your depression…”).
  • Instagram: 5 hashtags at most. Always include #LevelVibes.
  • Spelling: the app and App Store text use American spelling (color, favorite).

The app, in one paragraph

Level Vibes picks one thing to do based on the energy you’ve got. Make lists for anything (chores, meals, workouts, what to watch), split them into three levels, tap the level you can handle today, and it picks. Not feeling it? Pick again. Can’t face it? Just 2 minutes counts. Free on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch, with an optional one-time Plus Pack and no subscription. Made by one person, for days when getting started is the hardest part.
